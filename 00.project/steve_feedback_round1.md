## 13. Remaining SDA pricing tenure (New Build → Existing Stock)

Steve's concept: New Build pricing/classification has historically operated on a
20-year horizon from the relevant certificate of occupancy; once exhausted, pricing may
convert/revert to Existing Stock treatment under the applicable framework, which can
materially reduce SDA income — the capital-value impact depending partly on underlying
alternative-use value and other operating assumptions. A buyer may underwrite an asset
differently with, e.g., 18 years of New Build pricing remaining vs. 3 years remaining.

**STEVE_DIRECT_EXPERT_INPUT — NEEDS EVIDENCE CHECK (specific "20-year" figure).** The
abstracted public evidence notes currently in the pack (`PUB-02`, `PUB-03`) establish
that SDA pricing is a maximum-price framework distinct from guaranteed income and that
enrolment/design-category/participant-plan alignment conditions apply, but do not
themselves state the specific New Build → Existing Stock transition period. The
*concept* (remaining pricing-classification tenure as a value driver to be checked
per-dwelling) is used in the draft; the specific duration is not asserted as a
confirmed fact — see `CLAIMS_AND_EVIDENCE_IMPACT.md`.
