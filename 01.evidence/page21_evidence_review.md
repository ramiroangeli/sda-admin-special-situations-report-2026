## Current source verification

- How to enrol a home as specialist disability accommodation (SDA): https://www.ndis.gov.au/providers/home-and-living-providers/specialist-disability-accommodation-sda/how-enrol-home-specialist-disability-accommodation-sda (source date 2026-08-19). Steps 4 and 6: outgoing provider cancellation; incoming application assessed as new even after prior enrolment; mandatory documentation and relevant certifications. Ownership alone is not stated as an automatic trigger.

- How to manage specialist disability accommodation (SDA) vacancies: https://www.ndis.gov.au/providers/home-and-living-providers/specialist-disability-accommodation-sda/how-manage-specialist-disability-accommodation-sda-vacancies (source date 2026-08-11). Steps 2 and 4: limited eligibility for shared homes enrolled for 2 to 5 residents; specified departure circumstances, availability and notification conditions. Limits are up to 60 days for 2 to 3 residents or 90 days for 4 to 5, ending when filled. Claims relate to remaining eligible days after physical departure and require evidence. Never-occupied newly built homes are excluded. Public copy deliberately avoids procedural detail and directs eligibility testing.

- Pricing Schedule for Specialist Disability Accommodation 2026-27: https://www.ndis.gov.au/media/8844/download?attachment= (source date 2026-09-24). Effective 24 September 2026; 58 pages. Section 3.1(c)(vi), printed page 4, includes input tax credits among pricing factors. Appendix 1 Table 1, page 5: GST not paid, or paid with credits claimed. Table 5, page 10: GST paid without credits claimed. Corresponding base amounts differ. No calculation or universal income effect is published. This is the current schedule, not merely the older pricing-arrangements document also linked on the landing page.

- National Disability Insurance Scheme - GST-free supplies: https://www.ato.gov.au/businesses-and-organisations/gst-excise-and-indirect-taxes/gst/in-detail/your-industry/gst-and-health/national-disability-insurance-scheme (source date 2024-06-12). ATO requires all stated conditions for GST-free NDIS supplies: an effective plan, specified reasonable and necessary supports, written agreement, and a supply covered by the applicable determination. This is not authority that all owner/provider management services are GST-free. Test the actual supply and arrangements with specialist tax advisers. No sector statistic or definitive transaction tax conclusion is published.
