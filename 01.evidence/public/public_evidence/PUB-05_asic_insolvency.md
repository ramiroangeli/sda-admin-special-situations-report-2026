## What the sources establish
ASIC publishes aggregate corporate insolvency statistics including:
- Series 1: first external administration / controller appointment over a company;
- Series 2: all appointments, including first, subsequent and transitional appointments.

INFO 80 explains how the statistics should be interpreted.

## Supports
- ASIC can provide macro Australian corporate-insolvency context.
- The standard published series do not, by themselves, establish an SDA-specific insolvency trend.
