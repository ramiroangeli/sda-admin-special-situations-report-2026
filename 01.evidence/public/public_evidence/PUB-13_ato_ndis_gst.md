## Findings and limits
ATO requires all stated conditions for GST-free NDIS supplies: an effective plan, specified reasonable and necessary supports, written agreement, and a supply covered by the applicable determination. This is not authority that all owner/provider management services are GST-free. Test the actual supply and arrangements with specialist tax advisers. No sector statistic or definitive transaction tax conclusion is published.
