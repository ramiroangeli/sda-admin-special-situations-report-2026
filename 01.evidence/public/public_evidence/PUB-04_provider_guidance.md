# PUB-04 — NDIS Guide to providing SDA

## Primary source
NDIS — Guide to providing specialist disability accommodation (SDA)
https://www.ndis.gov.au/providers/home-and-living-providers/specialist-disability-accommodation-sda/guide-providing-specialist-disability-accommodation-sda

Retrieved: 2026-09-23.

## What the source establishes
The NDIS provider guide states, among other things, that:
- SDA participants must meet SDA eligibility / needs and funding criteria;
- newly built SDA requires design-standard certification, but certification itself does not guarantee enrolment;
- SDA providers must be registered and SDA homes must be enrolled;
- provider responsibilities are governed by the SDA Rules;
- provider/participant service agreements and participant rights are part of the operating framework.

## Supports
- Certification, enrolment, provider registration and participant funding are distinct concepts.
- SDA administration needs to preserve and verify more than title and physical property records.
