## What the public sources establish
The 2026 amending Act changed the statutory pathway for participant-requested unscheduled plan reassessments. The amended regime introduces conditions for a participant-requested reassessment and current NDIA guidance explains that participants seeking a reassessment outside regular timing need evidence of a significant and ongoing change in their situation/support needs.

## Supports
- The plan reassessment pathway changed in 2026.
- The public material supports describing the significant-change/evidence threshold.

## Does NOT establish
- that the reform has stopped SDA approvals;
- that a particular SDA funding request would have succeeded under the prior regime;
- that the reform is the sole or primary cause of observed SDA funding friction.
