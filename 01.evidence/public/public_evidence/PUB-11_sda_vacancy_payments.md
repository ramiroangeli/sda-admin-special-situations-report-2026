## Findings and limits
Steps 2 and 4: limited eligibility for shared homes enrolled for 2 to 5 residents; specified departure circumstances, availability and notification conditions. Limits are up to 60 days for 2 to 3 residents or 90 days for 4 to 5, ending when filled. Claims relate to remaining eligible days after physical departure and require evidence. Never-occupied newly built homes are excluded. Public copy deliberately avoids procedural detail and directs eligibility testing.
