# PUB-02 — NDIS SDA Pricing Arrangements 2026–27

## Primary source
NDIS — The specialist disability accommodation (SDA) pricing arrangements
https://www.ndis.gov.au/providers/home-and-living-providers/specialist-disability-accommodation-sda-tools/specialist-disability-accommodation-sda-pricing-arrangements

Retrieved: 2026-09-23.

## What the source establishes
The NDIA describes the SDA pricing arrangements as setting the appropriate and reasonable **maximum prices** for SDA supports. The page also provides the 2026–27 pricing-arrangements document and SDA price calculator.

## Supports
- Maximum / enrolled pricing is not itself proof of actual property income.
- The price calculator is an expected-income tool for an enrolled SDA home, not evidence that a particular participant, dwelling or investor will receive that amount.

## Supports claims
- ADM-008-01
- ADM-008-03 (only in combination with internal actual-income evidence)

## Publication treatment
PUBLIC PRIMARY SOURCE.

## Guardrail
Do not describe the maximum price as guaranteed income, sustainable NOI, collected income or market value.
