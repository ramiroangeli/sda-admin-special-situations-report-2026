# INT-01 - Market Intelligence Patterns

## Scope
Synthesis of SDAHC Market Intelligence records. Raw records remain in Notion and are not provided to Claude Code.

## Internal evidence profile
The database contains a mixture of market meetings, peer/network intelligence, deal activity, research/report notes and industry news. A material portion of the records are tagged as Risk Signals and Market Thesis.

## Repeated patterns observed

### 1. Funding conversion friction
Multiple independent market contacts have reported increasing difficulty converting participant housing need into timely usable SDA funding. This is an observed market signal, not a quantified national approval-rate statistic.

Publication treatment: P3/P4, aggregate only.
Supports: ADM-004-03, ADM-004-04, ADM-009-01.

### 2. Capital recycling and exit activity
SDAHC has observed recurring owner discussions involving sale, recapitalisation, capital recycling, portfolio review and divestment preparation.

Publication treatment: P3/P4, aggregate only. Do not identify counterparties or describe confidential sale processes.
Supports: ADM-004-03, ADM-016-01, ADM-017-01.

### 3. Provider transition and displacement
Market activity shows that freehold transactions, ownership changes and restructuring can trigger a change of SDA provider or challenge incumbent management arrangements.

Publication treatment: P3/P4 plus P2 where supported by transaction documents.
Supports: ADM-010-03, ADM-006-02.

### 4. Valuation methodology is being reassessed
Market discussions with valuers indicate increasing scrutiny of occupancy, actual income, lease-up assumptions and alternative-use/terminal value rather than reliance on enrolled theoretical income alone.

Publication treatment: use public/professional valuation evidence as the factual base; use market intelligence only as corroboration.
Supports: ADM-012-02, ADM-012-03, ADM-014-01.

### 5. Location-specific risk matters
Recent pre-construction reviews and market discussions reinforce that barriers to competing supply, land economics, local provider depth and participant demand vary materially by location. Broad SDA demand cannot be assumed to validate a specific address or configuration.

Publication treatment: framework/interpretation, no named developer or location example unless public.
Supports: ADM-013-01, ADM-017-02.

### 6. Provider and SIL sustainability can create secondary property risk
SDAHC intelligence includes cases where operating pressure on service providers may affect participant continuity, vacancy, referral pathways and owner cashflow.

Publication treatment: aggregate only. Never name a provider or state insolvency/financial distress without public evidence.
Supports: ADM-010-03, ADM-018-02.

## Guardrails
Do not publish:
- named lender exposure;
- named provider financial concerns;
- buyer rankings;
- unverified distress estimates;
- the statement that legislation is stopping SDA approvals;
- a national vacancy rate derived from internal market commentary.
