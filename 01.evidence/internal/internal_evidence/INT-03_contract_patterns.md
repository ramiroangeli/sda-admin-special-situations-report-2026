# INT-03 - Provider / Head Lease / Management Agreement Patterns

## Scope
Synthesis from a current sample of SDA management agreements, provider engagement agreements and head leases held by SDAHC. This is not a statistical review of the whole market.

## Repeated commercial questions
1. Who is the registered SDA provider?
2. Who receives SDA payments?
3. Who receives RRC or other rent?
4. What fee is charged and what is the fee base?
5. Who sources and places participants?
6. Who bears vacancy exposure?
7. Who handles tenancy/residency administration?
8. Who pays routine maintenance and repairs?
9. Who pays capital works, certification and building-compliance costs?
10. What happens on sale or transfer?
11. What happens on material breach, loss of registration or insolvency?
12. Can rights/obligations be assigned or transitioned, and what participant consents or regulatory steps are required?

## Observed contract characteristics
- Provider duties can include enrolment administration, participant liaison, claiming, tenancy documentation and compliance processes.
- Owner obligations commonly retain property-level costs such as rates, insurance, repairs, structural works, capital works and property-specific certification.
- Some agreements expressly state that occupancy, SDA funding and investment returns are not guaranteed.
- Sale/transfer, insolvency and loss-of-registration clauses can materially affect continuity and transaction execution.
- Assignment/transition may remain subject to participant choice and control, legal requirements and regulatory/enrolment processes.

## Report implication
A provider agreement is not merely a management-fee document. It can determine control of income, participant relationships, transition mechanics, cost allocation and saleability.
