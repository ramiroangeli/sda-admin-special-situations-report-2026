# INT-04 - Income and Sustainable NOI Patterns

## Scope
Derived from actual rental schedules, sale-purpose P&L normalisation and SDAHC advisory work. All client figures removed.

## Observed pattern
Property accounts can show gross rent and net profit, but transaction analysis may still require further reconciliation before the income can be treated as sustainable SDA NOI.

## Typical bridge observed in practice
1. actual gross property income;
2. participant/place funding and rent reconciliation;
3. provider/property-management fees;
4. council rates, insurance, water and other property outgoings;
5. repairs and maintenance;
6. vacancy and lease-up allowance;
7. CAPEX/sinking-fund allowance;
8. one-off or non-recurring items;
9. normalised sustainable NOI.

## Key evidence-derived lessons
- The provider fee can be a material expense line and may vary by structure.
- Actual participant-level claimable income may need to be reconciled separately from the accounting P&L.
- A sale-purpose NOI may differ from accounting net profit after normalisation.
- Physical occupancy alone is insufficient to establish full SDA income.
- One occupied participant can contribute materially less income than another if funding or rental arrangements differ.

## Public wording boundary
Safe: accounting profit, theoretical SDA pricing and sustainable transaction NOI can differ.
Do not publish universal provider-fee, vacancy, operating-cost or CAPEX percentages from this sample.
