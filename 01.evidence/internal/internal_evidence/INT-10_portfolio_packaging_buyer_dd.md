# INT-10 - Portfolio Packaging and Buyer DD Patterns

## Scope
Derived from portfolio advisory, institutional investment material and SDAHC transaction feedback. No buyer identity or proprietary underwriting model is included.

## Buyer diligence themes observed
Sophisticated capital commonly focuses on:
- actual occupancy and participant status;
- sustainable income rather than enrolled potential;
- location-specific demand and supply;
- provider capability and contractual flexibility;
- operating expenses and maintenance/CAPEX;
- certification/enrolment position;
- alternative-use downside;
- transaction structure and transferability.

## Portfolio packaging lessons
- A portfolio should not automatically be sold as one line because it was financed or owned as one line.
- Strong and weak assets may have different buyer universes.
- Geography, operating state, provider structure and alternative-use characteristics can justify segmentation.
- Conversely, a stronger asset may sometimes support the marketability of a weaker but complementary asset.
- Packaging decisions should consider execution certainty and recovery, not headline presentation alone.

## Boundary
Do not disclose buyer rankings, individual buyer yield requirements, target returns, pricing formulas or named transaction feedback.
