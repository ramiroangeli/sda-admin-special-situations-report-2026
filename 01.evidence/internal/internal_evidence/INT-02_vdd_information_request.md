# INT-02 - SDAHC VDD / Information Request Pattern

## Scope
Derived from the current SDAHC information request checklist used in advisory and sale-preparation engagements.

## Information categories repeatedly requested

### Participant and occupancy
- accommodation/residency agreements;
- de-identified participant details;
- de-identified SDA funding evidence by place;
- vacancy history by place;
- current occupancy status.

### Financial performance
- income statements by enrolled place/property;
- expense statements excluding debt service;
- historical valuations;
- depreciation schedules where available.

### SDA enrolment and compliance
- enrolment confirmations;
- as-built SDA certification;
- SDA assessment/certification checklists;
- occupation certificates and relevant approvals;
- GST/enrolment status where relevant.

### Agreements and contracts
- SDA provider agreements;
- head leases / management agreements;
- SIL or tripartite agreements where applicable;
- supplier and maintenance arrangements.

### Property and capital items
- title and rates;
- plans and approvals;
- inventory/chattels;
- post-completion capital improvements;
- warranties;
- CAPEX or sinking-fund information.

### Insurance
- insurance policies;
- claims history.

## Report implication
The existing SDAHC VDD process can be adapted into an appointment triage structure. The critical change is prioritisation: an administrator/receiver needs to know which documents protect participant continuity, income, enrolment and control first, rather than simply requesting a complete vendor data room.
