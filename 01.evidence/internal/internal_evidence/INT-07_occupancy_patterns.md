# INT-07 - Occupancy and Vacancy Evidence Patterns

## Scope
Derived from historical occupancy schedules and current asset-level advisory records. The internal sample is not yet broad enough to support a national or market-wide vacancy statistic.

## Observed lessons

### Point-in-time occupancy can be misleading
Historical monthly records show periods of full occupancy interrupted by vacancies that persist for different durations. A single occupancy percentage does not show lease-up or re-tenanting time.

### Occupancy quality varies
Internal examples include:
- occupied and fully SDA funded;
- occupied but with materially lower income;
- occupied with non-SDA/market-rent support;
- vacant but with an identified participant/funding pathway;
- vacant without a credible near-term pathway.

### Vacancy data must be defined carefully
Do not combine:
- NDIA participants eligible/not-yet-using SDA;
- advertised vacancy listings;
- actual property occupancy;
- income-producing vacancy assumptions used by a valuer/buyer.

## Report implication
Occupancy quality and the expected time to stabilisation can matter more than a simple occupied/vacant label.
