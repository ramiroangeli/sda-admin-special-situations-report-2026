# INT-09 - Special Situations / Lender / Portfolio Advisory Pattern

## Scope
Derived from SDAHC special-situations portfolio mandates and advisory documents. All portfolio identities and figures removed.

## Workstreams already used in practice

### Portfolio and debt reconciliation
- align properties, titles, entities and loan structures;
- identify security/cross-collateralisation issues;
- confirm ownership and saleable structure.

### Income and operating review
- separate SDA and non-SDA income;
- assess quality/stability of income;
- review assignment/change-of-control issues.

### Provider strategy
- review current arrangements;
- assess whether provider transition can improve occupancy, compliance or saleability;
- plan transition if required.

### Asset performance and stabilisation
- identify vacant and underperforming assets;
- assess which assets justify stabilisation before sale;
- balance value uplift against holding cost and time.

### Debt and lender strategy
- understand release conditions and consents;
- align disposal sequencing with recovery requirements.

### Divestment decision
- portfolio sale;
- segmented/tranche sale;
- individual asset sale;
- alternative transaction structures where appropriate.

## Key evidence-derived principle
Advisory and divestment work may need to proceed in parallel where time pressure is material. A sequential model of 'fix everything, then consider sale' is not always economically rational.
