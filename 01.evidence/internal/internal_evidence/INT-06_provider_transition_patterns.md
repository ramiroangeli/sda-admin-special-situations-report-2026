# INT-06 - Provider Transition / Mixed Portfolio Pattern

## Scope
Derived from a live portfolio restructuring in which different properties sat in different operating states. Details removed.

## Mixed states observed within one portfolio
- enrolled and SDA occupied;
- enrolled but vacant;
- enrolled with SIL-related/private lease arrangements;
- enrolled with ordinary residential tenancy arrangements;
- not enrolled and operated under ordinary property-management arrangements.

## Transition tasks observed
- maintain current enrolment/management until replacement pathway is ready;
- coordinate incoming registered provider arrangements;
- obtain required participant consents and preserve choice/control;
- execute new SDA/service/tenancy documentation where needed;
- coordinate NDIA / NDIS Commission notifications or enrolment steps;
- transfer property/participant records;
- reconcile final property-level receipts and accounting;
- sequence transition to avoid unnecessary gaps in housing or income.

## Report implication
Changing an SDA provider across a portfolio can be a staged operational and regulatory transition, not the equivalent of simply replacing a conventional property manager.
