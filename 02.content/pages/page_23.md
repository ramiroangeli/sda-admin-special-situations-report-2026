Specialist commercial vendor due diligence before sale is a core value-protection step
for SDA assets. A conventional property sale process may fail to identify or resolve
SDA-specific value drivers before the market prices the asset. Specialist SDA
Commercial Vendor Due Diligence can distinguish between impairment that is remediable
before sale and impairment that should instead be reflected in the realisation
strategy — it is not a guarantee of optimal value, and this report does not claim to be
one.

Some of the diagnostic and transaction work described in this report benefits from
specialist SDA input — not because it is legally required, but because SDA-specific
commercial judgement is not always available inside a standard property or
restructuring process.

SDAHC provides specialist SDA transaction advisory, commercial vendor due diligence and
market intelligence, including:

- Triaging an SDA-exposed appointment in its first days.
- Reconciling actual income to sustainable NOI.
- Reviewing the property and provider position together, rather than separately.
- Setting portfolio strategy where assets are heterogeneous.
- Preparing a data room and sale-readiness position.
- Running a special-situations divestment process.

Specialist input is generally most valuable before income assumptions, valuation
expectations or sale strategy become fixed.
