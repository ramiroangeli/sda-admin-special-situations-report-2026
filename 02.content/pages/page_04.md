This report assumes property, restructuring and lending expertise — not existing SDA
sector knowledge. The terms below recur throughout the report and are defined here for
quick reference.

**SDA / NDIS**
- **SDA** — Specialist Disability Accommodation: housing funded under the NDIS for
  participants with extreme functional impairment or very high support needs.
- **NDIS** — National Disability Insurance Scheme: the national scheme that funds
  supports for eligible people with disability, including SDA.
- **NDIA** — National Disability Insurance Agency: the Commonwealth agency that
  administers the NDIS and enrols and pays for SDA.
- **Enrolled Dwelling** — a dwelling formally enrolled with the NDIA as meeting SDA
  requirements, making it eligible to attract SDA payments.
- **SDA Provider** — an organisation registered to deliver, and claim payment for, SDA
  at an enrolled dwelling.

**Design Categories**
- **HPS** (High Physical Support), **FA** (Fully Accessible), **IL** (Improved
  Liveability) and **Robust** — four specialised SDA design categories, each addressing
  different physical, sensory or behavioural-safety needs.
- **Basic** — SDA design category for housing without specialised design features but
  with other SDA characteristics. Basic is available for Existing Stock, not New Build
  dwellings.

**Housing / Support**
- **SIL** — Supported Independent Living: NDIS-funded daily-living support, funded and
  delivered separately from SDA.
- **RRC** — Reasonable Rent Contribution: the tenancy-style rent a resident pays a
  provider, separate from and in addition to SDA payments.

**Property / Economics**
- **New Build** — SDA classification for dwellings meeting the applicable New Build
  criteria, including the relevant certificate-of-occupancy requirements from 1 April
  2016. **Existing Stock** — SDA classification for a dwelling that does not meet the
  New Build definition, priced on a different basis.
- **NOI** — Net Operating Income: income remaining after operating costs, before debt
  service and capital items.
- **CAPEX** — Capital Expenditure: capital-works spending, distinct from routine
  operating costs.
- **Vendor Due Diligence (VDD)** — a vendor's own pre-sale investigation of an asset's
  position; this report's central concept, Specialist SDA Commercial Vendor Due
  Diligence, is the SDA-specific version of it.

**Geography / Market**
- **SA4** — Statistical Area Level 4: an ABS geography classification commonly used to
  describe SDA supply and demand by region.
