**Before a realisation strategy is fixed, all relevant information needs to be
collated and reconciled.** Where the appointee does not have specialist SDA expertise
internally, a specialist commercial adviser should be considered for this work. Page 16
established what needs to be reconciled; this page establishes the order — and the
go/no-go judgement.

Specialist SDA Commercial Vendor Due Diligence verifies the information held is
accurate and consistent, and reconciles it against actual claims, funding and occupancy
records. Before broad market launch, material issues found this way should be resolved
where practical, or clearly identified and documented. Targeted market sounding or
transaction preparation may run in parallel where timing pressure makes a sequential
process uneconomic — consistent with page 8's observation that advisory and divestment
work don't always have to wait on each other.

Reasonable remedial and value-protection actions follow from that diagnosis, not
before it. Once the asset or portfolio is market-ready — data room prepared around the
resolved (or clearly flagged) position, defensible economics set using the NOI bridge
from page 12, not the headline price from page 9, and the initial packaging strategy
(page 17) established while retaining the ability to adapt it as credible market
evidence emerges — a specialist SDA sales agency should be appointed for a targeted
market process. A specialist agency should understand SDA portfolio sales, funding and
occupancy economics, provider dynamics, buyer mandates, and in-use versus
alternative-use value — the same commercial ground this report has covered.

Launching before material issues are resolved or documented doesn't remove the
uncertainty — unresolved uncertainty can reappear in pricing, conditions, Buyer Due
Diligence requests or execution time.
