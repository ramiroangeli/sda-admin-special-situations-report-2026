# PAGE 12 — FROM ACTUAL INCOME TO SUSTAINABLE NOI

**Reader question:** What does the asset actually earn on a sustainable basis?

Accounting profit is not automatically transaction NOI. A sale-purpose reconciliation
starts from actual or annualised property income — not the headline price from page 9
— and works down through the costs of running the asset day to day: provider and
management fees, property outgoings (rates, insurance, water), routine repairs and
maintenance, and occupancy normalised across a period rather than read off a single
point in time. Non-recurring operating income or expenses are treated as normalisation
adjustments where appropriate, depending on their nature. The result is normalised,
sustainable NOI — not the accounting P&L, and not the maximum SDA price from page 9.

Sustainable NOI is not the same as post-CAPEX cash flow. Known capital expenditure or
capital works, capital reserves where adopted, and lease-up or stabilisation capital
requirements are considered separately from normalised NOI — they affect underwritten
earnings and price, but they are not routine operating costs.

No single percentage applies to any of these steps across every asset — each has to be
derived from the specific dwelling's own record, not assumed from a rule of thumb. This
reconciliation is precisely what the diligence process tests before a sale, not
something a headline P&L can substitute for.
