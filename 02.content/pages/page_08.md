**Reader question:** What should I do next?

**Round 2 renumbering note:** was page 7 — shifts to page 8. Content unchanged besides

Page 7 covers the first 48 hours. This page sets the sequence for the full first
month — in substance, this is what Specialist SDA Commercial Vendor Due Diligence looks
like once a specialist adviser (page 7) is engaged.

**Secure** — protect participants, income, enrolment, property and records.
**Reconcile** — establish what is actually being claimed, collected and spent, against
what the file says should be happening. **Diagnose** — identify which links in the
page 6 chain are broken, and why (page 14). **Stabilise** — close the gaps worth
closing before any transaction. **Value** — test the current valuation assumptions and
establish the relevant value reference points, rather than relying on a
pre-appointment valuation (page 13). **Decide** — hold, stabilise further, sell as-is,
or restructure the provider relationship (page 18).

Treat this as the default reading order, not a rule to follow lock-step. Commercial
diagnosis and divestment preparation can run in parallel once time pressure is
material — waiting to "fix everything" before considering a sale is not always the
economically rational choice. Page 20 returns to this trade-off directly.
