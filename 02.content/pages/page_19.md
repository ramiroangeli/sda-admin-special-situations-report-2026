Occupancy percentage by itself can be insufficient for a recovery decision. Two
portfolios — or two multi-place assets — can report the same headline occupancy rate
and carry very different income risk, depending on what stands behind that number.
Headline occupancy is an operating statistic; Specialist SDA Commercial Vendor Due
Diligence tests the quality, sustainability and value relevance of the occupancy behind
the headline rate.

Five practical states are worth separating: occupied with aligned, evidenced funding;
occupied with uncertain funding; occupied with temporary or non-SDA income support;
vacant with a credible participant or funding pathway; and vacant with no credible
pathway. Each implies a different starting point for the stabilise/hold/sell decision on
page 18.

Occupancy rate alone can conceal material differences in income quality and
stabilisation risk.

**Visual — five-state triage matrix (unchanged):**

```text
1. Occupied + aligned/evidenced funding   → lower immediate remediation requirement
2. Occupied + uncertain funding           → verify before relying on income
3. Occupied + temporary/non-SDA income    → income quality requires review
4. Vacant + credible funding pathway      → potential stabilisation case
5. Vacant + no credible pathway           → re-test the underlying SDA proposition
```
