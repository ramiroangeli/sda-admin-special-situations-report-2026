**Round 2 change type:** REVISED. Reordered per Steve's second review: Provider /
Contracts becomes the first box, not paperwork for its own sake but because the first
commercial/safeguarding action is establishing who the current SDA provider is, what
governs the relationship, whether the provider (or a related entity) is itself under
distress, and whether participant housing continuity is at risk. The remaining five

genuinely time-critical) — Steve's own reordering is treated as sufficient direction,
and Q2 is retired from the review manifest; see `../review_manifest.yaml`.

The first 48 hours are not about completing full due diligence — they are about not
losing ground that is hard to recover later.

**If the appointee is not experienced with SDA, research and appoint a specialist SDA
commercial adviser early.** SDA carries funding, provider and participant dynamics a
generalist property or insolvency process is not built to read — early specialist input
protects options that are otherwise easy to lose in the first days.

Six things need eyes on immediately. **Provider / Contracts.** Identify the current
registered SDA provider and the agreements that govern the relationship. Engage with
the provider immediately to understand participant continuity, housing risk, claiming
access and whether any provider transition issue exists. Where the entity in
administration is the provider, or related to it, assess whether an independent
provider transition is required and obtain specialist and legal advice where
necessary — engage constructively with the current provider in the meantime. This does
not imply an appointee can simply replace a provider: legal, regulatory and participant
choice-and-control requirements still apply. **People.** Identify participant
continuity and housing/support risks immediately — protecting vulnerable participants
is a distinct, earlier priority than securing a vacant commercial building. **Income.**
Identify what is currently being claimed and where the cash lands; preserve access to
claiming, occupancy and funding information before systems access changes.
**Enrolment.** Confirm current enrolment status and any known change or re-enrolment
requirement. **Property.** Secure the physical asset — insurance, access, condition.
**Evidence.** Start preserving records before anything is moved or lost.
