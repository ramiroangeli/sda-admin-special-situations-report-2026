# PAGE 22 — SDA APPOINTMENT / REALISATION DECISION TREE

**Reader question:** What should I do next?

**Round 2 renumbering note:** was page 21 — shifts to page 22 (the former page 22,
"Before a Realisation Strategy Is Fixed," is retired — see
`../../00.project/changelog.md` for the retirement decision and the full old→new page
map). Content otherwise unchanged besides cross-reference renumbering.

Start with one question: is verified sustainable income currently being produced?

If yes, test whether funding, occupancy, provider position and enrolment are all
evidenced. If they are, test hold against a performing-SDA sale. If any of them isn't,
treat the position as unresolved and return to reconciling and diagnosing.

If not, the question becomes which source of impairment (page 14) is driving it — and
each source points to what to test next, not to a fixed outcome. Operational prompts a
test of whether remediation is economic; provider/contractual prompts a test of
transition or contractual restructuring; capital structure prompts a test of security
position, release conditions and transaction structure; fundamental asset prompts a
re-test of the SDA proposition against the alternative-use position (page 13).

The eventual pathway follows from what that testing finds — not automatically from the
diagnosis alone.

**Visual — decision tree (unchanged):**

```text
Is verified sustainable income being produced?
│
├── YES
│   └── Are funding, occupancy, provider position and enrolment evidenced?
│       ├── YES → Test: Hold vs. Performing-SDA Sale
│       └── NO  → Reconcile / Diagnose
│
└── NO
    └── What is driving the impairment? (page 14)
        ├── Operational           → Test: is remediation/stabilisation economic?
        ├── Provider/Contractual  → Test: provider transition / contractual restructure
        ├── Capital Structure     → Test: security, release conditions, structure
        └── Fundamental Asset     → Re-test: SDA proposition vs. alternative use (p.13)
```

**Pathway options** (determined by what the testing above finds, not by the source of
impairment alone): hold · stabilise · provider restructure/transition · sell as
performing SDA · sell as a special situation · segment portfolio (page 17) ·
alternative use / repurpose.
