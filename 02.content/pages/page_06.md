**Reader question:** What am I dealing with?

The chain below is what has to hold together for an SDA dwelling to convert into
transaction value. Enrolment alone does not create funding. Funding alone does not
create occupancy. Occupancy alone does not create claimable, collected cash.

The SDA Rules set these conditions out independently: a registered provider, an
enrolled dwelling, an eligible participant actually residing there, and alignment
between the dwelling's design category and the participant's plan. All must be
satisfied together — none substitutes for another.

A healthy asset shows alignment end to end. A distressed asset shows a break somewhere
in the chain — and the rest of this report is a method for finding exactly where.
