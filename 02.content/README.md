## Evidence discipline

Every new factual claim in this draft was checked against the existing evidence pack
**before** being written, not after — see `../CLAIMS_AND_EVIDENCE_IMPACT.md`. Where
Steve's own commercial observation went beyond what the evidence pack currently
supports (the >50% value-gap magnitude, the $10k/$80k illustrative example, the
three-year ~9,000 cohort trend, the 20-year New Build tenure figure, sale-below-
alternative-use public examples, remediation-cost valuation allowances), the *mechanism*
is published and the *specific magnitude* is either attributed, hedged, or flagged as an
open evidence gap — never invented. Each page's own "Evidence note" states exactly
which treatment applied.

## Terminology discipline

"Due diligence" was not blindly find-replaced. Four distinct terms are used
deliberately throughout: **Specialist SDA Commercial Vendor Due Diligence** (the
owner/appointee's pre-sale investigation — the report's new central value-protection
concept), **Buyer Due Diligence** (a purchaser's diligence specifically), **Commercial
Due Diligence** (investigation before a vendor pathway is selected), and **legal
review / legal due diligence** (genuinely legal content only). See
`../STRATEGIC_DIRECTION.md` §9 for the full rule.
